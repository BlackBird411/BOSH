About Bosh

Bosh is an embroidery brand built on one belief: the small details on what people wear and give say a lot about who made them.

We take an idea, a logo or a hand-drawn sketch and turn it into stitching that stays sharp wash after wash. Every piece goes through the same steps: design check, digitizing, stitching and a final inspection.

We work with businesses that need consistent branded wear, and with individuals who want something personal, made once and made properly.

  • PrecisionClean edges, even density and thread colors matched to your artwork.
  • Made to orderEvery piece is stitched for you, from a single item to a full batch.
  • Straightforward serviceClear quotes, clear timelines and a real person to talk to.

What we do

From a single gift to a full team uniform, we handle the design and the stitching in one place.

  • Logo embroideryCompany logos on polos, shirts, jackets and uniforms.
  • Caps and hatsFlat and raised stitching on caps, beanies and bucket hats.
  • Personalized giftsNames, initials and dates on towels, bags, bathrobes and keepsakes.
  • Patches and badgesCustom patches for teams, clubs and schools, finished with a clean border.
  • DigitizingWe convert your artwork into a stitch file, tuned for the fabric it will sit on.
  • Bulk and small ordersConsistent results whether you need one piece or several hundred.
